Reel Production Guide
Two Nations, One Legal Future
Reel Edit Reference: Every Hook, Clip and Caption
A complete, plain English guide to every reel in the series: exactly what the hook says, exactly which clip to use, and exactly what the caption should say. Built to hand straight to the editor, and to walk the client through.
60 reels  ·  4h 51m source recording  ·  7 week rollout  ·  9:16 video format
How Each Reel Entry Works
Every reel below is laid out the same simple way. There is no separate context screen anywhere in any reel. Context always comes through in on screen text or in what the speaker is saying, so nothing feels like a pause in the video.
1. The hook
The exact words that appear on screen the moment the video starts. This is written copy for the post, not something the speaker says.
2. The clip
Where in the original recording to find the footage, who is speaking, and the exact line to keep, plus how context comes through.
3. Editor's note
A practical instruction for that specific clip: pacing, a trim, or a moment not to cut away from.
4. Caption
The exact text to paste into the Instagram post description. Full event and session context lives here.

  counsels and professionals.
